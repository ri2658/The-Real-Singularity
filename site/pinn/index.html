Our PINN, running live in your browser

A PINN is a neural network that learns to obey the equation directly, instead of copying answers someone already computed. This one is small (8 layers of 20 neurons) and was trained on the thick fluid, the easy case where the jump in the middle stays gentle. Once trained, it answers instantly: pick a time and it draws the fluid's speed at every position.
