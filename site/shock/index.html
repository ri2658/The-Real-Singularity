Why Burgers’ equation forms a shock

Each characteristic carries its starting velocity u₀(x₀) = −sin(πx₀) in a straight line. Left of zero the fluid moves right and right of zero it moves left, so the lines collide at x = 0 at t* = 1/π ≈ 0.318.
