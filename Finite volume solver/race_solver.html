The race: a traditional solver vs. our PINN

This is the classic way to simulate the fluid from the previous page. It chops the line into many small cells and updates each cell's speed one small time step at a time (engineers call it the finite-volume method, and have used it for decades). There is no training: it just follows the rules of the equation.

Pick how thick the fluid is and how many cells to use, then press Run. The traditional solver computes the whole history on the spot, and our trained PINN, running in your browser, fills in the same 256 × 100 grid of points. Both are timed and compared with the exact answer (gray).
