Does a low loss mean a right answer?

A physics-informed network is trained by driving down a loss: how badly it breaks the equation at its training points. What we actually care about is the error: how far it is from the exact solution. Each dot below is one training run of our Burgers' equation network. Tap a dot to see how its loss and error moved during training.

What to notice. At ν = 0.003/π with evenly spread training points, the loss keeps dropping by over 200× while the error barely moves and ends at 35%. The same equation trained with extra points near the shock reaches a similar loss with an error of about 1%. The loss only checks the equation at the training points; it never sees the exact solution, so a small loss alone does not tell you the answer is right.
